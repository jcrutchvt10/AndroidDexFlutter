# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, direct, and constructive. Assume good intent, critique ideas and code rather than people, and keep issue/PR discussions focused on making RewriteDEX better.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, discriminatory language, or repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block participants whose behavior makes the project harder or unsafe to maintain.
